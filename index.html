English in 5 Minutes

    Minute 1 · Active listening

    Listen as many times as you like. Try to get the meaning from the sound, without translating in your head.

    Minute 2 · Repetition

    Say the sentence out loud 3 times. You don't need the app for this one.

    Tap a circle after each repetition (optional).

    Minute 3 · Creation

    Change one part of the sentence and say it. Example: “without saying a word” → “without making a sound”.

    Minute 4 · Personalization

    Use the same structure to talk about something real in your day.
